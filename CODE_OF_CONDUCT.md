# Community guidelines

This repo is a shared resource for ActionKit users at progressive advocacy organizations. The expectations here are simple.

## What we ask of everyone

- Be kind. Assume good intent in others.
- Keep feedback focused on the code, the docs, or the proposal — not the person.
- Respect that contributors have day jobs at understaffed nonprofits and may not respond immediately.
- Use plain language. Not everyone reading along is a developer.

## What's out of bounds

Behavior that's hostile, demeaning, or designed to drive people out of the project. If you wouldn't say it on a Slack channel at a serious advocacy org, don't say it here.

## How to flag a problem

Open a private message to the repo maintainer by emailing the contact listed on [campaign.help](https://campaign.help), or open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/CampaignHelp/ak-mailing-blocks/security/advisories/new) if you'd prefer the GitHub-native path.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block accounts that don't fit these expectations. We'd rather not, so please make it unnecessary.

## Scope

These guidelines cover issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other interaction in this repo. They do not cover anything that happens outside it.
